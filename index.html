Founder/Director's Welcome Address

Welcome to Sahel College of Health Sciences and Advanced Studies Gusau.

It gives me immense pleasure to welcome you to our institution — a community-based and non-profit college established with the vision of making quality health sciences education accessible and affordable to all, especially the less privileged and vulnerable members of our society.

At Sahel College, we strongly believe that education is a powerful tool for transformation, empowerment, and community development. This belief continues to guide our mission of supporting vulnerable populations, including orphans, and students from underserved communities across Zamfara State and beyond. Our commitment is not only to educate but also to restore hope, dignity, and opportunities for a better future.

Since our establishment, we have remained dedicated to providing affordable and standard health sciences education in an environment that promotes academic excellence, discipline, compassion, and professionalism. We understand the growing demand for qualified healthcare professionals in our communities, and we are proud to contribute toward building a stronger and healthier society through education.

Sahel College is an ideal destination for students seeking quality and practical health sciences training. Our institution is blessed with competent, experienced, and highly qualified tutors who are passionate about teaching, mentoring, and guiding students toward successful careers in the healthcare sector. Through modern teaching methods, practical exposure, and moral guidance, we strive to produce graduates who are knowledgeable, skilled, and committed to serving humanity.

As a non-profit institution, our focus goes beyond financial gain. We are driven by the desire to uplift communities, support vulnerable youths, and create opportunities for individuals who may otherwise be unable to pursue their educational dreams. We continue to work with community leaders, development partners, organizations, and well-meaning individuals to expand access to education and strengthen healthcare manpower development within our region.

I warmly invite you to become part of our growing academic family. Whether you are a prospective student, parent, partner institution, donor, or stakeholder, we assure you of our unwavering commitment to excellence, integrity, and community service.

Together, we can build a brighter future through education, compassion, and service to humanity.

Thank you for visiting our website and I look forward to welcoming you personally to Sahel College of Health Sciences and Advanced Studies Gusau.

Warm regards,
Nuhu Bashir Khalil

About Our College

Sahel College of Health Sciences and Advanced Studies, Gusau is a community-based, non-profit tertiary institution established to expand access to health sciences education for vulnerable populations, particularly the less privileged.

The college is driven by a strong commitment to equity, compassion, and community development, recognizing that education especially in health related fields can transform lives and strengthen communities. By offering accessible and affordable training, the college empowers individuals who might otherwise be excluded from higher education to become skilled health workers and agents of positive change.

Our Mission

The mission of the college is to provide accessible, affordable, and quality health sciences education, especially for disadvantaged and underserved individuals, through effective teaching, practical training, and community engagement.

The college is committed to:

  • Expanding access to education for vulnerable and marginalized groups.
  • Training competent, ethical, and compassionate health professionals.
  • Promoting preventive healthcare, health education, and nutrition awareness.
  • Providing hands-on practical and clinical training.
  • Encouraging a strong culture of community service and social responsibility.

Our Vision

To become a leading community-based and non-profit health sciences institution recognized for transforming lives through inclusive education and for producing competent health professionals dedicated to improving community health and wellbeing.

Philosophy

The college is founded on the belief that health and education are fundamental human rights, and that empowering vulnerable individuals with knowledge and skills leads to sustainable community development.

  • Education should be inclusive and accessible to all, regardless of background.
  • Healthcare training must emphasize community-based and preventive approaches.
  • Should combine knowledge, skills, and compassion in practice.
  • Should be service-oriented and socially responsible.

Our Academic Programs

Empowering the next generation of healthcare professionals with specialized training.

Higher Diploma in Public Health (Part-Time)

Duration: 2 Years

Advanced training for health professionals seeking to specialize in population health management and policy.

Diploma in Public Health Technician

Duration: 3 Years

Focused on technical skills required for environmental health, immunization, and disease surveillance.

Diploma in Nutrition and Dietetics

Duration: 3 Years

Comprehensive study of human nutrition, food science, and therapeutic diet management.

Diploma in Community Health Extension Workers (CHEW)

Duration: 3 Years

Preparing workers to provide primary healthcare services and health education at the grassroots level.

Certificate in Community Health Extension Worker (JCHEW)

Duration: 2 Years

Foundational training for junior health extension workers to support community medical initiatives.

Our Nutrition and Dietetics department is dedicated to training professionals who understand the critical role of nutrition in health promotion, disease prevention, and therapeutic management.

Students gain hands-on experience in our state-of-the-art nutrition laboratory and participate in community nutrition programs.

Department of Public Health
